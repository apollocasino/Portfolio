# Fluid Machine Dissection (FMD)

## Project Overview

Our team is conducting a Fluid Machine Dissection
to better understand the mechanical design,
components, and operating principles of a fluid machine.

## Project Goals

- Disassemble and document the machine
- Identify and analyze major mechanical components
- Understand the machine's operating principles
- Document engineering findings

## Project Status

In progress. Additional documentation and
photographs will be added throughout the project.
